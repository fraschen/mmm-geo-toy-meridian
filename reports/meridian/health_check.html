How is health score measured?

The Meridian model health score gives a unified view of model integrity by combining six individual health checks into one metric ranging from 0 to 100. While individual diagnostics provide granular insights into specific model components, the model health score provides an aggregate measure on a model's health for causal inference. How is calibration score measured?

The Meridian calibration score gives a view of the overall calibration health of your Meridian media and reach & frequency channels. The score combines the number of calibrated channels along with potential channel recommendations for the remaining channels. The calibration score is a factor in your overall model health score in the previous section. See Channel Calibration Recommendation and Score to learn more.
