Generated by `make eda` from `outputs/eda/eda_summary.json`. Meridian's own EDA report (default spec, prior draws only) is at [model_build/eda_default/eda.html](../model_build/eda_default/eda.html).

## Data sufficiency and integrity
- 40 geos x 156 weeks (2021-01-25 to 2024-01-15), 6240 rows, weekly steps of 7 days; balanced panel: True; duplicates: 0; missing values: 0.
- Negative values only in the centred controls (competitor_sales_control: 3238, sentiment_score_control: 3216); all spend, impressions, KPI, population and revenue-per-conversion are non-negative: True.
- Revenue per conversion is almost constant (mean 0.0200, CV 0.5%).
- Spend share: Channel0 18.5%, Channel1 14.3%, Channel2 5.5%, Channel3 40.0%, Channel4 21.7%.

![](figures/eda/spend_share.png)

## Spend vs impressions (CPM)
- Spend = impressions x a constant CPM in every channel: the largest deviation from the channel median is 0.14 parts per million (float rounding). CPMs: Channel0 7.33, Channel1 9.64, Channel2 7.43, Channel3 7.79, Channel4 7.79. Impressions therefore carry no information beyond spend, and Meridian's cost-per-media-unit outlier flags are rounding noise.

## National trend
- Linear trend 1.46% of mean revenue per year (HAC SE, Newey-West 4 lags: t = 2.59, p = 0.010; with 13 lags p = 0.007). Last 52 weeks vs first 52: +2.6%.

## Yearly seasonality vs chance
- Week-of-year means explain R2 = 0.360 of detrended national revenue. Pure noise of the same length gives 0.330 on average (95th percentile 0.418); p = 0.28 (iid null), 0.28 (AR(1) null, phi = -0.025). Per geo, 6 of 40 geos are significant at 5% (2 expected by chance). Seasonality detected: False.

![](figures/eda/seasonality_null.png)

## Noise floor
- Geo-week revenue: CV median 30.0% (range 21.5%-49.4%); lag-1 autocorrelation of detrended revenue median 0.007; MAPE of a 13-week moving average 23.1%.
- National weekly revenue: CV 6.9%, lag-1 autocorrelation -0.025, MAPE of a 13-week moving average 5.1%.
- Weekly revenue is close to white noise around a slow trend, at both levels.

## Common weekly variation (inputs for knots and residual variance)
- Week dummies explain 5.3% of within-geo per-capita revenue variance; a within-geo permutation null gives 2.5% (95th percentile 3.0%, p < 0.001). There is common weekly variation beyond noise, but it mixes baseline shocks with nationally timed media and controls, so the EDA cannot say how much of it a weekly baseline should absorb.
- Meridian's Automatic Knot Selection on the outcome picks 66 knots out of 156 weeks.
- Per-geo per-capita revenue sd vs population: Spearman -0.33; smallest/largest sd 0.60.

## Controls: confounders?
- Within-geo correlation with revenue: competitor_sales_control -0.20, sentiment_score_control -0.03, Promo +0.13, Organic_channel0_impression +0.03. Controls also correlate with spend (max |r| 0.55): competitor_sales_control with Channel0 +0.07, Channel1 +0.18, Channel2 +0.44, Channel3 +0.50, Channel4 +0.55. Both controls move with spend and with revenue, so they are confounders and must stay in the model.
